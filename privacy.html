Privacy Policy

Last updated July 29, 2026

connectEd is a project-collaboration platform: people publish projects, apply to join others, and find collaborators near them. This page explains what information connectEd collects, why, and how it's handled.

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you sign in with email/password or with Google, we receive your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and profile photo.
  • Profile details. Display name, headline, bio, skills, a profile photo and cover image, and an optional external chat link (e.g. a Messenger or WhatsApp link) you choose to add.
  • Location (optional). If you choose to share it, an approximate location — rounded to about a kilometer before it ever leaves your device — is stored so you can appear as a pin on the live member map. You can remove it at any time from your profile.
  • Projects and applications. Projects you publish, applications you send (including your message and any answers to application questions), and reviews you leave for people you've worked with.
  • Messages. Direct messages you send to other members through connectEd's messaging feature.

Google Calendar access

If you connect Google Calendar to schedule an interview, connectEd stores a long-lived refresh token so it can create a calendar event and Google Meet link on your behalf without asking you to sign in again each time. This token is used only to create interview events you explicitly schedule through connectEd, and is never used to read your existing calendar or any other Google data.

Payments

connectEd offers an optional paid membership (connectEd Plus). Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe — connectEd never receives or stores your card number. connectEd stores only a Stripe customer/subscription identifier, used to check whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

connectEd does not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your personal information to anyone.

Third-party services

connectEd relies on a small number of service providers to operate: Supabase (authentication and database hosting), Vercel (application hosting), Google (sign-in and Calendar/Meet integration), Stripe (payment processing), and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (turning a typed city name into coordinates when you share a location). Each handles data under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • You can edit or remove most profile information, including your shared location, at any time from your profile.
  • You can disconnect Google Calendar access from your Google Account's third-party access settings.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the address listed on the connectEd project page, or by reaching the site owner directly through connectEd.
